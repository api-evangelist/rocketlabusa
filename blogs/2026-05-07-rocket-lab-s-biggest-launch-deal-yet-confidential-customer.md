The multi-launch contract includes dedicated missions on five Neutron and three Electron rockets for a confidential customer with the launches baselined for 2026-2029 Long Beach, California. May 7, 2026. Rocket Lab Corporation (Nasdaq: RKLB), a global leader in launch services and space systems, today announced it has signed the largest launch contract in Company history with a confidential customer: a bulk order of dedicated Neutron and Electron launches that brings Rocket Lab’s total launch manifest to more than 70 missions and values the Company’s overall backlog at more than $2.2 billion.

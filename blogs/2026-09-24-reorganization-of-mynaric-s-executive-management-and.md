Munich, Germany. September 24, 2026 – Mynaric AG, a leading provider of optical laser communication terminals headquartered in Munich and subsidiary of Rocket Lab Corporation (Nasdaq: RKLB), announces a reorganization of the executive management and Supervisory Board. Andreas Reutter was appointed Chairman of the Executive Board of Mynaric AG by the Supervisory Board.

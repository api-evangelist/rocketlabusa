By bringing industry-leading satellite laser communication technology in-house Rocket Lab will strengthen its capability to serve the national security needs of multiple sovereign nations and commercial customers globally Long Beach, California. 30 March 2026. – Rocket Lab Corporation (Nasdaq: RKLB) (“Rocket Lab” or “the Company”), a global leader in launch services and space systems, today announced it has received regulatory approval to acquire Mynaric AG (“Mynaric”), a leading provider of laser optical communications terminals for air, space, and mobile applications.
